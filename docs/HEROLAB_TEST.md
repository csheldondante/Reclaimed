# Hero lab: test card (round 128)

Open this on the phone:

https://csheldondante.github.io/Reclaimed/herolab/?skipmenu=1&level=herolab

It opens on a menu, "Hero lab: what to try". Each button is one test. Tap it, play, then tap **☰ tests** (top row)
to get back to the menu. The line under the top bar says what is on, in plain words, and the question. The top
bar is one row; **⚙ settings** opens the chips (station, hero, camera, the bike's handling). The address bar keeps
the test, the hero and every chip, so you can copy the link and tell me what you thought.

The camera on every test is cut and tilt (your pick from round 120), except the tour. The cut now only cuts things
that hide something, only on the near side of the hero, and rises like a bowl away from the hero, with dithered
edges. The tour tries your new camera idea, "auto": it stays isometric, zooms in where tall things stand round you
and pulls back on open ground, and tilts only by cliffs. It is a variant to try, not the default yet.

## Things to try

1. **Bike, canyon: try "as it is", then tight, carve, slows down, much more grip.** Steer it yourself (touch the
   stick and the autopilot lets go). Which one makes the turns? Which one still feels like the bike?
2. **Bike, city, carve.** Can you ride round the blocks without hitting the walls?
3. **Bike, hills.** Does the camera stay still on gentle hills now? (The cut should not fire there at all.)
4. **Bike, cliffs.** Ride round the massif. Does the cut show you the bike and the turn ahead? Is the bowl's rise
   right, too steep or too flat?
5. **Knight, crowd.** Two waves come. Swipe twice fast to lunge through the crowd. Do you slip through and come out
   the other side? Is the lunge long enough now?
6. **Knight, guards and lungers.** Guards block from the front. Lungers wind up, then charge. Can you tell when to hit
   them?
7. **Knight, jump course and lock on.** Jump up the steps and over the gap, and swipe in the air to jump attack. Does
   the jump feel right? Then tap a raider to lock on. Do your slashes and arrows go where you want?
   **Parry, three ways:** try Parry button, Parry swipe and Parry hold against the lungers. Which feels best?
   **Bow and hook:** switch weapons. Is aiming the bow easy? With the hook, tap a raider to yank it in, or tap the
   ground to zip there. Is it fun? The forest and canyon tests ask whether you can see the knight.
8. **The physical drive (movement as one physics for every hero).** Knight, hill: run at the slope from far back,
   then tap Standing start. Does the running start up the hill feel right? Knight, raiders, physical: does the knight
   still feel snappy? Bike, canyon, physical: does the bike feel like the same bike? Brute, a heavy body on the knight:
   it charges and turns wide. Is that fun? Lich, hill: does it still float?
9. **Bike and knight, the tour (auto zoom).** One loop: a field, shrubs, low trees, tall woods, the canyon's cliff
   wall, a city street. Does the zoom follow what's around you? Closer in the tall woods, pulled back on the field
   and among shrubs? Does it pump in and out? Does the tilt by the cliff help? (The camera chips have **auto+town**
   too, which also tilts between buildings. Try it on the city street and say which you like.)
10. **Horse (new).** Canyon, city, forest, tour. Steer it yourself. Does the horse take the canyon for you without
    feeling on rails? Does it stop before walls? Try the assist chips under settings: strong, light, off.
11. **Lich, raise the dead / raiders / cliffs.** Tap Crowd, then Smite and Raise. Is building an army fun? (We still
   owe the lich a design talk. This is only to remind you how it plays now.)

## The phone benches

Heroes and cameras, about 6 minutes:
https://csheldondante.github.io/Reclaimed/herolab/?skipmenu=1&level=herolab&tutorial=0&herobench=1

One tap on the link, then leave the phone alone; it keeps the screen awake. It rides the canyon six times: each hero
with the plain follow camera, then with cut and tilt. Then the bike rides the tour twice: plain follow, then auto. It
reloads between runs. At the end, tap Download and send me the JSON. It tells us what each hero, the cut and the
auto camera cost on your phone.

The knight, a scripted fight with no enemies, raiders, then a mixed wave:
https://csheldondante.github.io/Reclaimed/herolab/?skipmenu=1&level=atlas&tutorial=0&play=knight&station=1&knightbench=1

Both keep the screen awake. At the end of each, tap Download and send me the JSON.

## What to send back

For each, one line is plenty: "carve felt best in the canyon", "the lunge is too short", "the cut is too low at the
cliffs". The link from the address bar says exactly which variant you were on.
